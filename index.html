How to Use Step Desk

Checklists: Tap an item to check it off. Tap the star to pin it to the top of its section. Use the search bar to quickly find items.

Customization: Tap EDIT at the bottom to add or remove your own sections and items. Tap the app title or category headers to rename them.

Notes: Type directly into the "Notes…" field below any item to save specific details (they save automatically).

Tools: Access aviation calculators, references, MAR tracking, and the suggestion box by tapping Tools at the bottom.
